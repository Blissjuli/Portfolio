ABOUT ME

Full Stack Developer. Fashion Designer. Baker.

My Story

I'm a passionate Full Stack Developer from Nigeria who builds modern websites and web applications with clean, scalable code. I'm also the founder of Bliss Juli Limited, where I bring the same craft and care to fashion design and baking.

What I Do

I design, build and deploy complete web solutions — from pixel-perfect frontends to solid backends and databases. Through Bliss Juli Limited, I also create custom outfits and delicious cakes & pastries for clients across Nigeria.

My Mission

To use technology to solve real problems beautifully — while building a creative business that empowers my community through software, fashion and food.

Why Clients Love Me

Reliable communication, clean and maintainable code, on-time delivery, and genuine care for every project and every client — technical or creative.
